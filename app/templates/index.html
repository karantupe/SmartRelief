WHY SMARTRELIEF?

When resources are limited, every decision matters.

During a disaster, drones may carry only a limited amount of medicine, food, water, and emergency equipment. SmartRelief explores how optimization can help select a valuable, capacity-feasible combination of supplies.

◇

Limited payloads
